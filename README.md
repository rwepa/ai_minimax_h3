## 2.中文MINIMAX提示詞
場景：皇家音樂廳——真正的洩密者

角色參考圖與服裝設定：
1.	維拉（Vera）：使用 女1_正面.png 作為角色參考。維持原有臉部特徵、湛藍色眼睛、髮型及身形。身穿海軍藍色配金色刺繡的華麗皇家禮服，手持一份帶有手寫註釋及特殊編碼符號的小提琴樂譜。
2.	伊莎貝拉（Isabella）：使用 女2_正面.png 作為角色參考。維持原有臉部特徵、長黑色捲髮及身形。身穿翠綠色華麗長禮服，站在維拉對面。
3.	雷昂皇帝（Leon）：使用 男1_正面.png 作為角色參考。維持原有臉部特徵、髮型及身形。身穿黑色與酒紅色相間的皇家軍服，配有精緻金色裝飾。
4.	諾亞（Noah）：使用 男2_正面.png 作為角色參考。維持原有臉部特徵、髮型及身形。身穿象牙白與銀色相間的皇家服裝，手中拿著從古董小提琴盒中發現的隱藏手稿。

場景描述：
場景位於宏偉壯麗的皇家音樂廳。金色水晶吊燈懸掛於高聳的天花板，溫暖的金色燈光照亮整座大廳。舞台及周圍空間充滿莊嚴、緊張且戲劇性的氣氛。
維拉站在畫面中央偏左的位置，手持一份帶有特殊編碼註釋的古老小提琴樂譜。伊莎貝拉站在她正對面，原本優雅的神情逐漸變得僵硬。雷昂皇帝站在兩人附近，穿著黑酒紅色皇家制服，震驚地注視兩人。諾亞則從一側走向前方，手中展示從古董小提琴盒中發現的隱藏手稿。
四位角色必須清楚呈現，人物臉部、服裝、身形及髮型與參考圖片完全一致。

影片動作與時間安排（總長 7 秒）：
•	0–1.5 秒：維拉公開密碼樂譜。 維拉站在皇家音樂廳中央，將手中帶有編碼註釋的小提琴樂譜緩緩舉起，轉身面向伊莎貝拉。她神情冷靜而堅定，目光直接注視伊莎貝拉，彷彿正式提出指控。
•	1.5–3 秒：諾亞拿出關鍵證據。 諾亞從側面穩定地走上前，將從古董小提琴盒中找到的隱藏手稿展示在眾人面前。手稿外觀古老，帶有與密碼樂譜相呼應的特殊符號及手寫註記。諾亞神情嚴肅，動作堅定。
•	3–4.5 秒：雷昂皇帝震驚。 雷昂皇帝看見密碼樂譜與隱藏手稿，臉上露出明顯的震驚。他緩慢轉頭望向伊莎貝拉，眼神充滿難以置信與懷疑。
•	4.5–7 秒：伊莎貝拉的謊言即將被揭穿。 鏡頭緩慢向前推進，逐漸將焦點從四人對峙的畫面轉移至伊莎貝拉的臉部特寫。她原本鎮定的表情逐漸僵硬，眼神閃爍，嘴角緊繃，臉色變得蒼白，試圖掩飾內心的恐懼。最後定格在伊莎貝拉緊繃、震驚且無法掩飾心虛的臉上。

鏡頭與構圖要求：
採用電影級寫實攝影風格。開始以四位角色同時入鏡的中遠景呈現皇家音樂廳的對峙場面。鏡頭平穩、緩慢地向前推進，清楚捕捉維拉舉起樂譜、諾亞展示手稿、雷昂轉頭看向伊莎貝拉的連續動作。最後以伊莎貝拉的臉部特寫作為結尾，營造強烈的懸疑與戲劇張力。
人物動作自然，情緒表演細膩，不使用誇張的肢體動作。四位角色在整段影片中保持外觀一致，不得交換位置或改變服裝。

旁白（Voice-over）：
「真正洩密的人，不是演奏這首曲子的人，而是知道它原本怎麼寫的人！」
旁白使用沉穩、低沉、富有戲劇張力的成熟男性聲線，以自然的台灣國語發音呈現。語氣堅定、具有揭露真相的力量。旁白不需要任何角色對嘴，不可讓畫面中的人物誤以為正在說這段台詞。
建議旁白在影片約第 3 秒開始，配合諾亞展示證據及雷昂轉頭的動作，於影片結尾前完成。

影像規格：
1920×1080，16:9 寬畫幅，7 秒，電影級寫實質感，皇家宮廷場景，華麗金色吊燈，溫暖金色燈光，細膩人物表情，戲劇性明暗對比，淺景深，精緻服裝細節。

負面提示詞：
不得改變四位角色的臉部特徵、髮型、膚色、身形及服裝；不得交換角色身份；不得讓維拉放下或丟棄樂譜；不得讓諾亞拿錯物品；不得讓隱藏手稿變成小提琴；不得讓雷昂走到伊莎貝拉身後或離開畫面；不得讓伊莎貝拉提前離場；不得出現多餘人物或重複人物；不得出現手指變形、手稿變形或不合理的肢體動作；不得出現快速晃動、突兀變焦或不必要的鏡頭切換；不得出現字幕、浮水印、標誌或亂碼文字。

## 3.英文MINIMAX提示詞(實際輸入用)
Scene: The Royal Concert Hall — The Real Traitor Is Revealed

Character References and Costume Details:
1.	Vera: Use 女1_正面.png as the exact character reference. Preserve her original facial features, vivid blue eyes, hairstyle, skin tone, and body proportions. She wears an elegant navy-blue royal gown with intricate gold embroidery and holds an annotated violin score containing mysterious coded markings.
2.	Isabella: Use 女2_正面.png as the exact character reference. Preserve her original facial features, long black wavy hair, and body proportions. She wears an elegant emerald-green royal gown and stands directly opposite Vera.
3.	Emperor Leon: Use 男1_正面.png as the exact character reference. Preserve his original facial features, hairstyle, and body proportions. He wears a black and dark burgundy royal military uniform with refined gold decorations.
4.	Noah: Use 男2_正面.png as the exact character reference. Preserve his original facial features, hairstyle, and body proportions. He wears an ivory-white and silver royal outfit and holds the hidden manuscript discovered inside the antique violin case.

Scene Description:
The scene takes place inside a magnificent royal concert hall. Large golden crystal chandeliers hang from the high ceiling, casting warm golden light throughout the grand hall. The atmosphere is majestic, tense, and highly dramatic.
Vera stands slightly left of center, holding an ancient violin score covered with handwritten annotations and mysterious coded symbols. Isabella stands directly opposite her, her previously elegant expression gradually becoming tense and uneasy.
Emperor Leon stands nearby, wearing his black and burgundy royal uniform, watching the confrontation in shock. Noah approaches from one side, holding and presenting the hidden manuscript discovered inside the antique violin case.
All four characters must remain clearly recognizable and visually consistent with their respective reference images.

Action and Timing — Total Duration: 7 Seconds:
•	0–1.5 seconds: Vera reveals the coded score. Vera stands in the center of the royal concert hall and slowly raises the annotated violin score in front of Isabella. She turns toward Isabella with a calm but determined expression, looking directly at her as if formally confronting her.
•	1.5–3 seconds: Noah presents the crucial evidence. Noah steps forward from the side and reveals the hidden manuscript discovered inside the antique violin case. The ancient manuscript contains mysterious symbols and handwritten annotations that correspond to the coded markings on Vera's violin score. Noah looks serious and resolute.
•	3–4.5 seconds: Emperor Leon reacts in shock. Leon notices the connection between the coded score and the hidden manuscript. His expression changes to unmistakable shock and disbelief. He slowly turns his head toward Isabella, looking at her with suspicion.
•	4.5–7 seconds: Isabella's deception is about to be exposed. The camera slowly pushes forward, shifting focus from the confrontation to a close-up of Isabella's face. Her composed expression gradually freezes. Her eyes become restless, her lips tighten, and her face turns pale as she struggles to conceal her fear. End on a dramatic close-up of Isabella's tense, shocked, and visibly guilty expression.

Camera and Composition:
Use cinematic photorealistic cinematography. Begin with a medium-wide shot showing all four characters confronting one another inside the royal concert hall.
The camera performs a smooth, slow push-in, clearly capturing Vera raising the coded score, Noah presenting the hidden manuscript, and Leon turning toward Isabella.
End with a dramatic close-up of Isabella's face, creating strong suspense and emotional tension.
All character movements must be natural and restrained. Avoid exaggerated gestures. Maintain strict character identity, costume, hairstyle, and facial consistency throughout the entire sequence.

Voice-over Narration:
"真正洩密的人，不是演奏這首曲子的人，而是知道它原本怎麼寫的人！"
Use a mature male voice with a deep, calm, authoritative, and dramatic tone. Deliver the narration in natural Taiwanese Mandarin pronunciation, with strong emphasis on the revelation.
The narration must be a separate voice-over, not spoken by any visible character. No lip-sync is required.
Begin the voice-over at approximately the 3-second mark, synchronized with Noah presenting the evidence and Leon turning toward Isabella. Complete the narration before the end of the video.

Visual Specifications:
1920×1080 resolution, 16:9 widescreen aspect ratio, 7-second duration, cinematic photorealism, magnificent royal palace interior, luxurious golden chandeliers, warm golden lighting, detailed facial expressions, dramatic chiaroscuro, shallow depth of field, and elaborate royal costumes.

Negative Prompts:
Do not change the facial features, hairstyles, skin tones, body proportions, or costumes of any of the four characters. No character identity swapping. Vera must not drop or discard the score. Noah must not hold the wrong object. The hidden manuscript must not turn into a violin. Leon must not move behind Isabella or leave the frame. Isabella must not leave the scene. No extra or duplicated characters, no distorted fingers, no malformed manuscript, no unnatural body movements, no shaky camera, no abrupt zooms, no unnecessary camera cuts, no subtitles, no watermarks, no logos, and no garbled text.
